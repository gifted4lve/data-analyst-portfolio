# Restaurant Sales Performance Analysis (Nigerian Dishes)
Power Bi capstone project analyzing a full year (2024) of restaurant order - 3,650 orders across 10 Nigerian Dishes.

## Tools Used
Excel (Power Query) . Power BI . DAX

## What This Project Covers
- Da)ta cleaning (data time local fixes, duplicate/type validation)
- Data modeling (relationship between Orders and Menu tables)
- KPIs cards, revenue & volume analysis by dishes, time, day and month
- Interactive dashboard with slicers (payment type,item, date)

## Key Insights
- White Rice With Beef Stew is the top revenue earner (1,004,400), despite Fried Rice having more orders - proving popularity & profitability.
- Friday is the busiest and highest-earning day of the week.
- Revenue climbs steadily from September to December, suggesting holiday = season demand.
