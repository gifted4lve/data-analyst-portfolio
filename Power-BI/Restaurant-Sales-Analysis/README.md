&#x20;**Restaurant Sales Analysis – Power BI**



**----- *Project Overview***



This project analyses restaurant sales data for 2024 using 'Microsoft Power BI'. The aim is to transform raw restaurant order data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making.



The analysis focuses on sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product performance, revenue trends, and other key business metrics.



**---- *Project Objectives***



The main objectives of this project were to:



\* Analyse overall restaurant sales performance

\* Identify top-performing products

\* Understand sales trends and patterns

\* Analyse customer ordering behaviour

\* Identify the best-performing days and periods

\* Create interactive dashboards for business reporting

\* Provide actionable insights and recommendations



***----- Tools \& Technologies***



\* 'Power BI' – Data cleaning, data modelling, DAX and dashboard development

\* 'Power Query' – Data transformation and preparation

\* 'DAX'– Measures and calculations

\* 'Microsoft Excel / CSV' – Source data preparation.



***-----Data Analysis Process***



The project followed these main steps:-



1\. 'Data Collection'– Imported restaurant order and menu data.

2\. 'Data Cleaning' – Checked data types, missing values, inconsistencies and other data-quality issues.

3\. 'Data Transformation' – Prepared the datasets using Power Query.

4\. 'Data Modelling' – Created relationships between the relevant tables.

5\. 'DAX Calculations' – Created measures required for the analysis.

6\. 'Data Visualization' – Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard.

7\. 'Analysis \& Insights' – Examined the results and identified important business trends.

8\. 'Recommendations' – Developed recommendations based on the findings.



***Key Performance Indicators***



The dashboard presents important restaurant performance metrics, including:



\* Total Revenue

\* Total Orders

\* Total Quantity Sold

\* Average Order Value



***Key Insights***



The analysis identified several important patterns in the restaurant's sales performance, including:



\* Identification of the highest-revenue dishes.

\* Identification of the strongest-performing days of the week.

\* Analysis of product and menu-item performance.

\* Identification of sales patterns that can help guide business decisions.



&#x20;***Business Recommendations***



Based on the analysis, the restaurant can:



\* Focus promotional activities on high-performing products.

\* Use sales trends to plan inventory and staffing.

\* Promote products with lower sales to improve their performance.

\* Identify high-demand periods and prepare resources accordingly.

\* Continue monitoring sales performance through an interactive dashboard.
