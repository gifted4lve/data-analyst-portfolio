# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis – Excel, SQL & Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes 5,000 e-commerce orders to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional trends, customer behavior, and payment methods.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Measure overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing product categories and regions.
- Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
- Identify top-performing products and customers.
- Understand sales performance by payment mode.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis and Pivot Tables
- **SQL / MySQL** – Data querying and business analysis
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations

🔍 Key Business Insights
Regional Performance

The North region recorded the highest sales among the four regions analyzed, making it an important market for the business.

Category Performance

Home Decor was identified as a leading category by sales, indicating strong customer demand for products in this category.

Payment Method

Net Banking recorded the highest sales among the payment methods analyzed.

Product Performance

Product-level analysis was used to identify products contributing strongly to overall revenue and to highlight products that may require additional promotional attention.

Time-Based Performance

Sales and profit varied across months and years. This indicates opportunities for the business to use seasonal patterns when planning inventory, promotions, and other resources.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

Focus on high-performing regions
Increase marketing and customer-retention activities in regions generating strong sales.
Prioritize high-performing categories
Maintain sufficient inventory for popular categories such as Home Decor while monitoring demand closely.
Improve weaker product performance
Products with lower sales can be reviewed for pricing, promotion, positioning, or bundling opportunities.
Use seasonal trends for planning
Historical sales patterns should be used to improve inventory planning, staffing, and promotional campaigns during high-demand periods.
Leverage customer and product insights
Customer and product performance data can support more targeted promotions instead of applying the same strategy across all customers.
Monitor payment behavior
Continue monitoring payment methods and ensure that popular payment options provide a smooth customer experience.
Use the Power BI dashboard for continuous monitoring
Management can use the dashboard to regularly monitor KPIs and identify changes in sales and profitability.
